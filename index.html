USER GUIDE

How to sell safely

  1. Choose the correct gift-card brand, region and currency.
  2. Enter the card denomination. Your saved name, email and contact number are attached automatically.
  3. Upload clear pictures. Do not expose redeemable codes or PINs in this form.
  4. Submit once and follow the order status in My orders.
  5. Save your Nigerian bank details in Account for payouts.

The 30-minute review deadline is recorded when an order is submitted. If a valid trade is not completed within that period, compensation eligibility is subject to review and the published business terms. No compensation is automatically credited.
